# Third-party notices

TrainerRelay is MIT licensed. Its Windows executable also includes Python, Tcl/Tk, Bleak, PyUSB, WinRT bindings, and their dependencies. PyInstaller provides the bootloader and packaging hooks under its distribution terms and bootloader exception.

Every release ZIP includes **THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt** with the bundled license texts, collected during the build. The ANT driver is a separate prerequisite and is not redistributed.

Upstreams: [Python](https://www.python.org/) · [Tcl/Tk](https://www.tcl.tk/) · [Bleak](https://github.com/hbldh/bleak) · [PyUSB](https://github.com/pyusb/pyusb) · [PyWinRT](https://github.com/pywinrt/pywinrt) · [PyInstaller](https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller)

Related bridge projects are linked in the [development notes](docs/development.md#references-worth-reading). They are references, not bundled dependencies.
